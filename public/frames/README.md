# Frames Directory

Add your Diwali frame PNG images here!

## Required Files:
1. `landscape.png` - Frame for horizontal/landscape photos
2. `portrait.png` - Frame for vertical/portrait photos

## Format:
- File format: PNG with transparent center
- The center should be transparent so the photo shows through
- The decorative border/frame should be on the edges
- Recommended size: 1080x1080 for portrait, 1920x1080 for landscape

## Tips:
- Make the decorative elements bold enough to be visible
- Ensure good contrast with typical photo colors
- Leave enough transparent space in the center for the photo content
- Festive borders with diyas, rangoli patterns, or traditional motifs work great!

Once you add the frames, the app will automatically overlay them on photos based on orientation.
